How to play

Take turns dropping discs. Line up four of your colour across, down or diagonally.

Click or tap a column. On touch screens, slide to aim and lift to drop.

Keyboard

  • 1–7drop in that column
  • ←→choose a column
  • Enterdrop in the chosen column
  • Uundo
  • Nnew game
